Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jake Field Wholesaling (“we,” “us”), a sole proprietorship based in Little Rock, Arkansas operating at fieldhomebuyers.com, collects, uses, and protects your information when you text us, email us, or otherwise contact us about selling a property.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, and mailing address.
  • Property information, such as the property address, its condition, and details you share about your situation.
  • Message records, such as the content and time of text messages and emails you exchange with us, and your opt-in and opt-out history.
  • Public records about properties, such as county ownership and tax records.

How we use your information

  • To respond to you, discuss your property, and make you an offer.
  • To send text messages you have agreed to receive, and to honor opt-out requests.
  • To keep the records needed to run our business and comply with the law.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you text our keyword (such as CASH) to (501) 596-7081, you agree to receive conversational text messages from Jake Field Wholesaling about your property. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers (such as our text messaging platform) that deliver messages on our behalf.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, our text messaging and email providers), with parties you ask us to involve (for example, a title company handling your closing), or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and out of emails by replying UNSUBSCRIBE. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing jake@fieldhomebuyers.com.
